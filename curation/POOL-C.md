## Method & blocks

- Sources: VGMaps section index pages fetched 2026-11-01 (GBA, GB-GBC, PC, C64, PSX, PS2, ColecoVision, Amiga, MSX, AppleII, DS, ZXSpectrum, TG16, CPC, AtariST, N64, X68000, Lynx, Intellivision, VirtualBoy, 3DS, PSP, Mac, X1, PET, Archimedes, Arduboy, Wii, Neo-Geo, NGP-NGPC, WS-WSC, Atari8-Bit, PC-98, PC-FX, CD-i) + the earlier enumeration for Arcade / Atari 2600 / 5200 / 7800. VGMaps has no separate Game Boy or Game Boy Color section — both live in its combined "GB-GBC" atlas; picks from it are labeled "Game Boy".
- No 403/429 blocks were hit on VGMaps. Sections deliberately not mined: Switch / Switch 2 / PS3 / PS4 / X360 / Xbox / GCN / WiiU / Android / iOS / Mobile / J2ME (3D-era or phone catalogs whose "maps" are overwhelmingly 3D-level diagrams/screenshot stitches that fail the rendered-tiles bar); Sega sections (Genesis, Master System, Game Gear, Saturn, Dreamcast) belong to tranche B; NES/SNES to tranche A. The PC-88 section index uses a markup variant that yielded no parseable rows (3 games — noted, not chased).
- Bar enforcement beyond name/dims filters, from inspection: all "(In-Game Map)" diagram screens, multi-area sheets (stage spans, "Stages/Rounds" all-area sheets), annotated "Solutions", and 3D-era Sonic PC titles (screenshot stitches of 3D levels) were pruned; "(Unmarked)" rips were preferred where offered.
- View tags are heuristic (franchise/genre + map-name keywords): side = side-view levels, top = overhead/RPG/maze, other = fixed-screen arenas, vertical shmups, isometric/mixed.
- Arcade/Atari honesty note: most Atari 2600 maps on VGMaps are sparse; only full-game layouts whose structure reads clearly at downscale were kept (e.g. Pitfall!-style multi-screen composites and Berzerk-style arenas). Single-screen arcade picks are mostly maze/arena layouts that read as complete places.
