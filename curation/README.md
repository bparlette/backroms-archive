# curation/ — what's here, and what was left out

This folder holds the decision-grade record of the Nov 1, 2026 map-first curation: the four candidate pools (`pool-a.json`–`pool-d.json`, 1,709 verified candidates total), the combined 18,183-entry bar-passer pool (`pool.json`), the four tranche reports (`POOL-A.md`–`POOL-D.md`), the merge audit (`REPORT.md`), the resulting catalog (`proposed_catalog.json`), the alternates bench (`bench.json`), and the merge/select/build/verify scripts that produced them (`merge.py`, `select.py`, `build_catalog.py`, `verify_merge.py`).

## Deliberately left out (still on disk in the working directory `~/workspace/worlds-game/map-first/`)

- **Tranche working directories** (e.g. `tranche-c/`, the per-tranche candidate/verify intermediates like `pool-b-candidates.json`, `pool-b-verify.json`, `d_nesmaps_*.json`, `candidates_a.json`) — these include **large raw HTML scrapes of the source sites' index pages** and step-by-step verification dumps. They are bulk process residue, not decisions; the pools + reports capture every conclusion drawn from them.
- **Eyeball image folders** (`eyeball/`, `eyeball-b/`, `eyeball-merge/`, plus loose `d_eyeball_*.jpg` samples) — downloaded map images used for visual spot-checks during curation. They are binaries (excluded from this archive by design — images are referenced by URL) and were working aids; the eyeball verdicts are written up in the tranche reports and the merge `REPORT.md`.
- **Per-tranche builder scripts** (`build_pool.py`, `build_pool_b.py`, `select_a.py`, `select_verify_b.py`, `finalize_a.py`, `sheets_a.py`, `views_a.py`, `views_map.py`, `assemble.py`, `verify.py`) and run logs (`verify.log`, `verify_in.json` / `verify_out.json` / `verify_topup.json`, `selection.json`, `union.json`, `picks.json`, `games_a.json`, `d_existing_*.json`, `d_metroid_*.json`, `d_verify_out.json`) — reproducible intermediates. The final selection pipeline (merge/select/build/verify) is archived; the intermediate candidate files can be regenerated from the source sites with the archived reports as the guide.
